**The evidence layer under your AI workflow.**

ashaveri builds tools for independently verifiable AI evidence. Signed receipts
bind request and response hashes to a model identifier; open verification tools
let you check those records yourself.

Our focus is evidence continuity: records that remain checkable when models,
providers, or reviewers change.

## Start here

- **[Explore the code](https://github.com/ashaveri/ashaveri)**  
  Receipt formats, a TypeScript SDK, command-line verification, and conformance vectors.
- **[Read the protocol](https://github.com/ashaveri/ashaveri/blob/main/docs/receipt-spec.md)**  
  The signed receipt format, verification rules, and what each field means.
- **[Try the interactive proof](https://ashaveri.com/proof)**  
  Follow two AI answers through their receipts, then change the evidence and run verification in your browser.
